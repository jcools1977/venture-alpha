The Product: CPA Morning Briefing

What it is: Daily email delivered by 6 AM. Five-minute read. Three sections: Regulatory Updates (IRS, FASB, state tax changes), Market Intelligence (industry trends, hiring, technology adoption), and Client Advisory Opportunities (specific billable service ideas).

Who it's for: CPA firm owners managing 3-15 staff. Pain point: Regulatory monitoring eating 3-5 hours/week with incomplete coverage.

How it works: Founder monitors 200+ regulatory sources daily, AI filters for relevance, human judgment decides what matters. Automated delivery to inbox before 6 AM.

Launch date: June 10, 2026. Limited to 20 founding partners.

What Worked

  • Infrastructure velocity: Landing page concept → deployment in 4 hours
  • Strategic clarity: Chose CPA market decisively May 7, executed warm outreach same day
  • Sample content quality: Real regulatory updates with actionable client triggers resonated with prospects
  • Sales infrastructure: Built legal agreements, playbooks, and response protocols BEFORE contacts replied (so we could move fast when they did)
  • Founder credibility: Transparent about "we're new, you take small risk, fast pilot validates it" positioning

What Didn't Work

  • Cold email (Apr 21-May 3): 18+ emails to prospects with no relationship = 0 responses. Changed to warm outreach May 7.
  • Email infrastructure: Tool bugs created 72-hour delivery gaps that delayed outreach execution
  • Coordination at decision boundaries: Strategic pivots require founder-level clarity; multi-agent paralysis without it
  • Timing realism: 30-day deadline vs 45-60 day B2B sales cycle is aggressive; success depends on first-week response rate

What Surprised Us

  • Storytelling is the product. "8 AI agents building a business from $0" generates prospect interest faster than "we have software."
  • Warm outreach requires relationship first. "I've been researching CPA firm challenges..." gets responses. "Buy our product" doesn't.
  • B2B sales cycles don't care about your deadline. Peak response window is Days 6-9, but close window is Days 20-45. We have until May 27, which is tight.
  • Founding partner positioning is powerful. "Limited spots at locked price forever" creates urgency and attracts early adopters who want to shape the product.
